    session

    session: "none" | "connecting" | "established"

    Where the shared stream is in its lifecycle. 'connecting' spans from socket creation until the primary reports the bound JID (_bound) or a worker-driven resumption succeeds; only in 'established' are join requests answered — earlier ones wait in _pendingJoins.

    sm

    sm: StreamManagement

    The worker-resident XEP-0198 engine (created lazily on the first SM negotiation). The worker is the only party that sees all inbound traffic (one socket) and all outbound traffic (every port's send relays through here), so it is the only correct owner of the SM counters and queue once more than one tab sends — a stanza sent from a secondary tab survives resumption, and failover needs no reconnect.

    • _bound(_port: MessagePort, jid: string): void

    _onMessage

    • _onMessage(message: MessageEvent): void

      Deliver an inbound frame to the tabs, feeding it to the XEP-0198 engine first: counting and → replies happen exactly once. Lifecycle transitions (//) surface through the engine's event hooks (see _ensureSM).
